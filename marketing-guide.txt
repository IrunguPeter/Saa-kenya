=========================================

Snap-refresher on what you're selling
-------------------------------------
- Price point (KSh 500-5,000) -> impulse-buy range, perfect for social.
- M-Pesa STK push -> frictionless local checkout, mention it everywhere.
- Affiliate program (10% commission, 30-day cookies) -> your built-in growth engine.
- Preorders & custom orders -> great content hooks.

TIKTOK (priority - best ROI for your niche)
--------------------------------------------
- Content formats: 15-20s "watch under KSh 2,000" styling videos, unboxings,
  before/after of a watch on wrist, "last 3 in stock" scarcity videos, ASMR of
  watch straps/cases.
- Post 1-3x/day. Use trending sounds + your niche keyword in the caption.
- Key tactic: enabling TikTok Shop isn't ideal for your flow - instead drive to
  site via link in bio and a "DM to order" CTA.
- Hook idea: "Every watch under KSh 1,000, tested for 30 days - full refund if
  it breaks."

INSTAGRAM (aesthetic + trust)
-----------------------------
- Feed: bright product flat-lays, lifestyle wrist shots, unboxing reels,
  customer try-on photos.
- Reels (same clips as TikTok), stories daily (restock alerts, flash sales,
  behind-the-scenes).
- Use the affiliate link as your bio CTA + the WhatsApp group link.
- Highlight reels: "Under 1K", "New Arrivals", "Custom Orders".

FACEBOOK (community + older/local shoppers)
---------------------------------------------
- Page + Groups. Your product suits gifting - run "gift guide" posts.
- Marketplace listing for each watch (free local reach).
- Boast M-Pesa payment + nationwide delivery in every post - that's your
  differentiator.

X / TWITTER (announcements + support)
--------------------------------------
- Use for: restock drops, flash sales, tech-y/corporate gifting angles, and
  quick customer support.
- Don't over-invest here; treat as broadcast channel.

WHERE TO PROCURE VERY CHEAP WATCHES (SOURCING GUIDE)
=====================================================

Goal: buy rock-bottom, then resell at your KSh 500-5,000 price points with a
healthy margin.

LOCAL (KENYA) - fastest, lowest hassle
----------------------------------------
- Jumia Kenya "Wholesale" section (jumia.co.ke) - generic men's quartz watches
  as low as KSh 241-420, many under KSh 1,000. Search "wholesale watches".
- Jiji Kenya (jiji.co.ke) - thousands of men's/women's watches from ~KSh 399.
  Direct suppliers advertise wholesale from KSh 100-250 per piece.
- Nairobi CBD wholesale markets:
  - Kamukunji / Star Shopping Mall - watch wholesalers, mix-and-match cases.
  - Pramukh Plaza - known for quality watch wholesale options.
  - Timepiece Palace (Accra Towers) - ~KSh 150/pc wholesale (4 pcs min),
    ~KSh 250 retail.
  - Top Time Shop (CBD, opp. International Life House) - original brands
    (Casio, Citizen, Fossil) with warranty, if you want an authentic tier.
- Worthwhile budget brands already popular in Kenya: Curren, Naviforce, Skmei,
  Wwoor, Q & Q, Winner, Casio. "Luxury-inspired" looks (Rolex/AP/Ondea style)
  sell well at budget prices - just be careful about counterfeits.
- Example wholesale shops: Bansi Suppliers (Ruiru, 0722 353 974),
  Pablo Gift Shop, saazone-kenya (genuine Casio).

ONLINE (CHINA) - cheapest per unit, longer shipping
----------------------------------------------------
- 1688.com / Yiwugo / YiwuBuy (Yiwu wholesale market online) - the actual source
  of most TikTok cheap watches. Prices ~CNY 8-40 (~KSh 140-700). E.g. fashion
  quartz watches from ~$1.34/pc, delisted/live-stream styles lower.
- Alibaba (apparel.alibaba.com) - B2B wholesale, MOQ often just 1 piece on
  watches; "homage" brands like Denvosi from ~$9.70-11/pc. TikTok/Temu grades
  retail $3-7.
- AliExpress - fine for small test orders/dropshipping; $3-20 per watch.
- Temu / SHEIN - good to see what prices/resellers charge, sometimes cheaper to
  buy direct from them in bulk than to source elsewhere.
- Hoobuy / similar China forwarding services - their "spreadsheet" lists 7,000+
  cheap Chinese products weekly including watches; handy for resellers.
- Cargo/agents (cargoclear.com is a common clearance route) for bulk import;
  budget freight, clearing, and 16% VAT + import duty on watches.

TIKTOK-SPECIFIC SOURCING PLAY
------------------------------
- Watch TikTok Shop "Deals" / $1 price-unlock drops as a signal for what's
  trending and at what price buyers bite. Brands like Denvosi blew up this way.
- Reverse-source: find a watch that's viral on TikTok -> search its name on
  1688/taobao/Alibaba -> usually 40-70% cheaper than the TikTok retail price.
- Use viral TikTok sellers as retail-price benchmarks for your own Store.

DO'S AND DON'TS
-----------------
- DO order small samples (2-5 pcs) before bulk; check movement, strap quality,
  and that they're not dead-on-arrival.
- DO verify any "waterproof/luminous" claims - test them or it hurts your brand.
- DON'T sell obvious counterfeits (fake Rolex/AP logos) - platform takedowns,
  chargebacks, and liability risk.
- DON'T buy purely on price: 1 bad batch of 100 watches = 100 refunds.
- Remember shipping/import costs add 20-40% to landed cost; keep landing cost
  under ~KSh 350-400 for your entry KSh 500 price tier (or bundle 2-3 for a
  "combo" impulse offer).
